How to read a card

Every peptide, eight questions.

How does it work?Mechanism
The biology in one line — which receptor, signal or pathway the peptide acts on. If the mechanism is only proposed, the card says so.
What does it solve?Goals and conditions
The problems it is studied for. This is what the research looked at, not a promise it will work for you.
Which part of ageing?Hallmark
Science groups ageing into 12 hallmarks — such as worn-down telomeres, tired mitochondria and senescent "zombie" cells. Each peptide is tagged to the hallmark it targets.
How would I know it is working?Biomarkers
The blood tests, scans or scores that show whether there was a need to begin with, and whether anything changed. No baseline, no way to judge.
How much proof is there?Evidence
Ranked from approved medicine, through human trials, to early or mixed results, down to animal-only data. Open the evidence badges below for detail.
How is it taken?Delivery
Injection under the skin, oral, nasal spray, topical, or clinic-only IV and surgical use. Many peptides break down in the gut, which is why injection is common.
Who is it for?Segment
Medical — treating a diagnosed condition. Life Quality — sleep, recovery, mood, performance. Longevity — slowing biological ageing.
What are the risks?Safety
Known side effects, who should avoid it, and where the safety data simply does not exist yet. Always read this part.
What does each evidence badge mean?
Approved
Approved by at least one national regulator for a specific condition. That approval does not cover longevity, performance or off-label use.
Human trials
Tested in people in controlled studies with reasonably consistent results, but not approved for the uses listed.
Early / mixed
Small, older, observational or conflicting human data — or trials that failed. Promising at best, unproven at worst.
Animal only
Studied in cells or animals only. Effects and safety in humans are genuinely unknown.

The LifeScan AI routing logic

From a person to a plan, in five filters.

Nothing is suggested from a goal alone. Every route passes five gates, and a clinician signs off the result.

1SegmentWho is this for?
We first place you in Medical, Life Quality or Longevity. This decides which peptides are even considered — a longevity goal never routes to a prescription-only diabetes drug, for example.
2Hallmark, confirmedWhat is actually happening?
A goal is not enough. We look for evidence in your data that a specific hallmark of ageing is involved, and only route to peptides that target it.
3Baseline biomarkersWhat to test first
Each route lists the tests to order before anything begins, so change can be measured rather than guessed.
4Goal and timelineWhat success looks like
We set the outcome you care about and when it will be re-checked. No open-ended protocols.
5ContraindicationsThe safety gate
Safety rules override everything above. A history of cancer, pregnancy, or conflicting medication removes options outright. A clinician reviews every result.
Our standard

Verified sources only.

LifeScan never points to grey-market or "research chemical" vendors.

What is a Certificate of Analysis?
A lab report for a specific batch. It should show identity (it is the right molecule), purity (how much is actually peptide) and contaminants.
Why does endotoxin testing matter?
Endotoxins are bacterial residues that can cause fever and serious reactions when injected. Anything injectable without an endotoxin result is excluded.
Why independent testing?
A certificate written by the seller is not proof. We require testing by an independent lab that can be traced and verified.
