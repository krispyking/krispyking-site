A note on the voice you hear

Narration uses a synthetic reconstruction of Oliver Ransford's voice, made with his family's consent, reading his own words.

Every word spoken by that voice is drawn from what he actually wrote — his books, his thesis, his letters, his 1972 commentary track. It is never scripted new dialogue, and it never speaks as a character.
