The Word of a Gentleman

How this story travelled before it reached you

A man wrote in a tent beside Lake Tanganyika in 1871. You are watching a film about him made by his biographer's grandson, with machines, a century and a half later.

Thirteen removes sit between his pen and your eyes. This page walks every one of them.

The film's title asks whether a gentleman's word can be trusted. It is only fair to ask the same of the film.

Why we're telling you this

Every layer is honest about its own uncertainty. That is the most a gentleman's word can offer.

Nothing on this line is neutral, including the machines and including us. We have kept a written record of every ruling we made, every source we checked, and every place the AI was overruled or caught out. Where the film shows something no source supports, it is because someone chose to show it, and that choice is logged.
